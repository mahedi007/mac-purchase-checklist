Condition

Is it really new, or just sold as new? Fill in what you can see and Lidcheck tells you whether it is brand new, open box, Apple refurbished, third-party refurbished or second hand.

The verdict is an estimate from the evidence you enter, not proof. An Apple Store or Apple Authorized Service Provider can confirm history from the serial. Lidcheck is independent and not affiliated with Apple.

Keyboard

Click the keyboard, then press every key.

Hold fn to test F1 to F12. Press Command on its own, since shortcuts can trigger the browser.

Display

Full screen colors for dead pixels and backlight bleed.

Click or press Space for the next color, Esc to exit. Black shows stuck bright pixels, white shows dead ones.

Speakers

Turn the volume up and listen for buzz or rattle.

The sweep is the best rattle test. Listen closely at the low end.
